About Portal Music

What Is Portal Music?

Portal Music is a free music library for creators. We provide high-quality, royalty-free tracks that anyone can use in their videos, podcasts, streams, games, and commercial projects — at no cost, no sign-up required.

Our catalog covers a wide range of genres including Rock, Electronic, Hip-Hop, Jazz, Cinematic, Classical, Country, and more. New music is added regularly.

Our Mission

We believe great background music should be accessible to everyone — from hobbyist YouTubers to professional agencies. Copyright claims and licensing fees shouldn't stand in the way of creativity.

That's why every track on Portal Music is:

  • Completely free — no subscriptions, no hidden fees
  • Cleared for commercial use — safe for ads, client projects, and monetized content
  • Copyright strike-free — we will never claim or strike your videos on any platform

About Our Music

The music on Portal Music is original work produced for this platform. Tracks are created using a combination of professional music production techniques and AI-assisted composition tools. All music is owned by Portal Music and distributed under our free-use license.

Artists featured on Portal Music have agreed to distribute their music through this platform under these terms.

## 1. Set up the profile (10 min)
- **Profile picture:** `profile-picture.png`
- **Facebook cover:** `facebook-cover.png`
- **Category:** Musician/Band → or "Music Production Studio"
- **Username:** @portalmusic (or closest available); use the same one on both apps
- **Website / link in bio:** https://portal-music.com
- **Bio (Instagram, 150 chars):**
  > 🎵 Free music for creators
  > 470+ royalty-free tracks · No copyright strikes
  > YouTube · TikTok · Twitch · Podcasts
  > ⬇ Download free 👇
- **About (Facebook):**
  > Portal Music is a free library of 470+ royalty-free tracks for YouTube, TikTok, Twitch,
  > podcasts and indie games. No sign-up, no attribution required, and we never claim or
  > strike your videos. New music every week at portal-music.com.
- Facebook: add a **"Visit website"** button → portal-music.com
- Instagram: switch to a **Professional (Creator) account** (free) to see stats

## 3. Then start promoting
- Invite your own friends to like the Facebook page (Page → Invite friends)
- Share post 1 to your personal Facebook and Instagram story
- Join 3–5 Facebook groups for small YouTubers / content creators; when someone asks for
  music, answer helpfully with a link to a specific track, not just the homepage

## 4. Ongoing rhythm (2–3 hours a week)
Make the videos in the admin studio (`npm run admin` → **Reels & Videos**): pick a track, click
⏱ Now at the best part, choose 15–30 s vertical, Make video, then Download + Copy caption.

- **Mon:** "Music for your ___" Reel (gameplay/vlog clip + one track)
- **Wed:** before/after Reel (silent clip → same clip with music)
- **Fri:** "New this week" Reel (newest track + cover art)
- Always end captions with: *100% free for YouTube, TikTok & more. No copyright strikes. 🔗 portal-music.com*
- Reels > photos: short vertical videos get far more reach than images.
- Present Dem Bois, Avilyn Grace and Whiskey Pines as Portal Music's artist projects, not as real people.

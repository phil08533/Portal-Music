Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

Portal Music is a static website. We do not collect personal information, require account creation, or store user data on our servers.

The following data is stored locally in your browser and never sent to us:

  • Theme preference — saved in localStorage so your preferred theme persists
  • Recently played tracks — saved in localStorage for your convenience
  • Favorites — saved in sessionStorage and cleared when you close the tab

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense — displays advertisements on our site. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts our website. GitHub may collect technical information such as IP addresses in server logs.

Cookies

Portal Music itself does not set cookies. However, Google AdSense and other third-party services may use cookies to serve relevant advertisements. By using this site, you consent to the use of cookies by these third-party services.

For more information about how Google uses data, visit Google's Partner Sites Policy.

Children's Privacy

Portal Music is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

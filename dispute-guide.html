🛡️ You Are Protected — Here's What To Do If You Get a Claim

All music on Portal Music is free to use on any platform. If you receive a copyright claim or strike after using one of our tracks, it is wrongful and can be disputed and won. This guide walks you through exactly how to do that.

📖 What's the Difference: Claim vs. Strike?

  • Copyright Claim (Content ID) — An automated system flags your video. Your video stays up but ad revenue may be redirected. This is the most common situation and is easy to dispute.
  • Copyright Strike — A manual takedown request. More serious — three strikes can remove your channel. Rarer, but also disputable.

In either case, your use of Portal Music tracks is fully licensed and you have the right to dispute.

▶️ Step-by-Step: Disputing a Claim on YouTube

  1. Go to YouTube Studio → Content
  2. Find the video with the claim and click the claim details
  3. Click "Dispute" next to the claim
  4. Select "I have a license or permission from the rights holder"
  5. Paste the template below into the text box
  6. Submit — the claimant has 30 days to respond or the claim is released

📱 Other Platforms

  • TikTok — Go to Settings → Report a Problem, or use the in-app appeal link on the flagged video. Use the same template above.
  • Instagram / Facebook — Visit Meta's Rights Manager dispute form and attach the template.
  • Twitch — DMCA disputes go through Twitch's DMCA counter-notification process.
  • Any other platform — Use the template above. The key information is the license source (portal-music.com) and contact email for verification.

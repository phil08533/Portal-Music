## LinkedIn Post — Portal Music

---

Excited to share something I've been building: **Portal Music** — a free music library for creators, powered by AI-generated tracks.

As a creator, finding quality background music that's actually free to use (no copyright headaches, no licensing fees) has always been a pain. So I built the solution.

**What Portal Music offers:**
- Hundreds of tracks across Hip-Hop, Electronic, Cinematic, Lo-Fi, R&B/Soul, Pop, Ambient, Country & Folk, and more
- Featured AI-generated artists with full bios and catalogs — Avilyn Grace, Dem Bois, Whiskey Pines
- Browse by genre, search by mood or feel, download instantly
- Dark / light / sepia themes and a built-in player — no account needed
- Completely free to use for your content

**Under the hood:**
- Static site hosted on GitHub Pages — zero server costs, loads fast everywhere
- GitHub Actions automatically scans new audio files and updates the catalog on every push
- Fuzzy search, favorites, recently played — all client-side with no backend

Whether you're a YouTuber, podcaster, streamer, or filmmaker, Portal Music has tracks you can actually use.

Check it out: **portal-music.com**

I'd love feedback from creators and fellow builders. What genres or features would make this more useful for you?

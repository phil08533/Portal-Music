🎥 Streaming? Put Portal Radio on your stream

Free background music that's safe for Twitch, YouTube and Kick, plus a "Now playing" card for your viewers. Works in OBS and Streamlabs.

✦ Pro members can also stream the Pro stations and any of their own playlists.

🎧 Streaming a playlist? The link holds its tracks, so copy a fresh link after you change the playlist.

Preview ↗
  1. In OBS, click + under Sources → Browser → OK.
  2. Paste the link into URL. Set Width 1920 and Height 1080.
  3. Tick Control audio via OBS so you can set the music volume in your mixer. Click OK.
  4. The music starts by itself. To skip a song, right-click the source → Refresh.
